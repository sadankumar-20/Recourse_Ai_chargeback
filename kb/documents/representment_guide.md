## citation_rules
Every factual sentence in a representment must cite admitted evidence by
exhibit identifier. Policy statements may cite verified knowledge-base
sources. A citation is valid only when its quoted text exists verbatim in
the referenced source.

## structure
A representment should state the dispute reference and amount, present the
delivery and communication evidence in exhibit order, address the
cardholder's specific claim, and close with the requested outcome. Keep the
narrative factual; persuasion comes from verified evidence, not adjectives.
