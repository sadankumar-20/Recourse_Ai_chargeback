## delivery_evidence
For goods_not_received disputes, the representment must include a delivery
confirmation tied to the disputed order. Acceptable proof is a courier proof
of delivery or the courier's own tracking delivery record, and the airway
bill number on the proof must match a shipment registered against the order.
A delivery confirmation whose destination pincode differs from the order's
shipping pincode is not acceptable evidence of delivery.

## description_evidence
For not_as_described disputes, the strongest evidence is the customer's own
written acknowledgement of receiving or using the item, quoted verbatim from
communication that post-dates dispatch. Delivery proof alone does not rebut
a description claim; pair it with customer communication.

## duplicate_evidence
For duplicate-charge disputes, the representment must reconcile amounts: the
order total minus recorded refunds must equal the amount legitimately
retained. Any unreconciled difference must be refunded or escalated before
contesting.

## deadline_rules
Network deadlines are absolute. No representment may be submitted after the
respond-by timestamp, and cases within twenty-four hours of the deadline
require human review before any submission.
